The R0 rows as constructions.  Frame: a context is a small category C, a type in C is a functor C -> Set, and substitution along K: C -> D is precomposition K^*: [D, Set] -> [C, Set].  Lan_K is left adjoint and Ran_K is right adjoint to K^* (Riehl chapter 6, BELIEVED as above).  The kernel never computes a limit; each row's intro and elim are the unit and counit of that adjunction.

| Row | Category | Functor K (the shape) | Lan_K | Ran_K | Construction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pi_A | [Gamma.A, Set] to [Gamma, Set], Gamma.A the category of elements of A | the projection p: Gamma.A -> Gamma, precomposition is weakening | Sigma, coproduct over the fibre A(g) | Pi, product over the fibre A(g) | pointwise formula with (p down g) equal to the fibre when Gamma is discrete |

| nu_P | [omega^op, Set] to Set with the cochain 1 <- P 1 <- P (P 1) <- ... | the unique functor omega^op -> 1 | none used | limit of the cochain, the terminal P-coalgebra | needs P to preserve omega^op-limits, true for polynomial P on Set (connected limits), and unfold is the coalgebra universal property |

- Row notes.  Rows 2 and 3 are instances of "Kan extension along the collapse to 1 is a (co)limit" with a finite shape; row 1 is the same formula fibrewise; rows 4 and 5 replace the finite shape by omega and the shape carries P inside the diagram.  The polynomial P = Sigma_a X^(B a) is itself Lan_f then Ran_g then h^* for B -> A -> 1 (BELIEVED, settle: Gambino and Kock, Polynomial functors and polynomial monads, section 1).  Dependent elimination for mu_P is the initial algebra in the slice over mu_P (BELIEVED, settle: Hermida and Jacobs, Structural induction and coinduction in a fibrational setting).
- Attack seam for R0.  Rows 1 to 3 are Kan extensions by construction with a finite shape.  Rows 4 and 5 are Kan extensions only through Adamek's chain, which the kernel cannot compute; what the kernel implements is the algebra or coalgebra universal property.  The smuggling attack should ask whether mu_P and nu_P are shapes or a fixpoint former in disguise, and whether the R0-AUDIT gate can see the difference.

## 11.  Findings for the panel (seven)

1. Bend 2 does not target HVM.  It emits one C file for clang, Metal or CUDA, or JS.  Its README calls native compilation slow.  The R2 denominator must name the Bend 2 target and stage, or it is undefined.  VERIFIED bend-readme.md:218,238 and bend-guide.md:584-585.
2. Bend 2 publishes checker bench inputs with Lean, Agda, Isabelle and Rocq twins but no per-line number in text.  Stage 0 can freeze bench/checker/*/main.bend and main.lean as the twin corpus.  VERIFIED bend-bench-checker.html.
3. No project runs Lean 4 compiled code on SP1 or RISC Zero.  lean-zkvm runs a Rust re-implementation of the kernel in a RISC Zero guest with the proof term as input.  VERIFIED zkvm-lean4-readme.md:20-28.
4. Neither CompCert nor CakeML has ELF emission inside the proof.  CompCert stops at the Asm AST and calls an external assembler; CakeML's verified artifact is the byte list wrapped by unverified assembly text and a C FFI basis.  VERIFIED compcert-Assembler.ml:20-31 and cakeml-export-riscv.sml:22-55.
5. A proved RV32IM instruction encoder exists: riscv-coq's decode_encode lemma.  A Lean 4 port is the cheapest route to a proved encoder; the ELF header stays gate-tested.  VERIFIED riscvcoq-DecodeEncode.v:10.
6. Juvix reaches RISC Zero through Rust source and cargo, the architecture the R2 trawl form forbids, and its erasure keeps a dynamic placeholder.  No prior language with Pi and inductives has a trace-erasure result on a zkVM.  VERIFIED juvix-risc0-cmd.hs:11-12 and juvix-RemoveTypeArgs.hs:26.
7. R0 rows 1 to 3 are Kan extensions along a finite shape by the pointwise formula; rows 4 and 5 rest on Adamek's omega-chain and the algebra universal property.  The attacks should test mu_P and nu_P as the smuggling seam.  BELIEVED, settle with Riehl chapter 6 and Gambino and Kock.
